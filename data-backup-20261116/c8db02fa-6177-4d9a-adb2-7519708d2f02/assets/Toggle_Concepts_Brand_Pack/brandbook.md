# Toggle Concepts — Brand Book v1.1

Text version of `Toggle_Concepts_Brandbook.pdf`. Section names follow the brand kit field catalog. Rules marked *proposed* need confirmation.

## 1. Brand name
Toggle Concepts. Product of Toggle, the motion design studio. After the first mention: "we". Never "ToggleConcepts", "TC" or "Toggle AI".

## 2. Brand description
A creative department by subscription. Senior creatives own the idea and art direction, our engine builds every version, experts check every file. Clients pay per concept; every version is included.
Audience: growing digital brands that run paid ads, and agencies that need extra capacity.
Slogan: "Scale your content, not your team."

## 3. Wordmark
TOGGLE in heavy caps; the two O's form one pill (the toggle). It is drawn, not typed: use the SVG files only.

## 4. Icon / symbol
The pill. App icon: white pill on the hero gradient. Hero object: the glossy orange ring (`logo/png/toggle-ring-gloss.png`).

## 5. Logo usage
Navy on light; white on navy, gradient and calm photo areas; black for one-color print only. Clear space: 0.5 × wordmark height. Minimum width: wordmark 64 px, lockup 120 px. In ads: top left inside the safe zone, about 3% of the shorter side.

## 6. Primary colors
Navy #0C2B3F (ink) · White #FFFFFF (paper) · Sky #B4E4FA (highlight) · Signal Orange #FF4F17 (one accent per screen).
Proportions: white/gray 55–60%, navy 25–30%, sky 10–15%, orange 1–3%.

## 7. Secondary colors
Gray #F1F1F1 · Muted #5A6B78 · Mist #BFD3E0 · Line #DDE3E8 · Deep navy #081F2E.
Gradients: hero #B9E7FA → #86AFC4 → #3A5B71 → #0C2B3F; light #F3FBFF → #B4E4FA.

## 8. Semantic / UI colors
Hover on navy #1D4A66 · Focus ring #2C7BE5. *Proposed:* success #1F7A4D, error #C8331A.

## 9. Display fonts
Inter Tight 500 and 600. Hero slogan 600 in caps.

## 10. Body fonts
Inter Tight 400 and 500. Buttons 700 in caps.

## 11. Monospace fonts
None in the brand. System monospace only for code.

## 12. Font files and licenses
`fonts/` Inter Tight TTF 400–700, SIL Open Font License 1.1.

## 13. Typography rules
Tight negative tracking on headlines, none on body. Sentence case; caps only for the hero slogan, labels and buttons. At most one word in orange. Numbers as digits.

## 14. Spacing
4 px base: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 48. Grid gap 20 px (12 px on phones).

## 15. Grid / layout
Bento of rounded tiles on a white page: 12 columns, max 1440 px, tile radius 32 px. Neighboring tiles alternate tone. Clickable = pill (999 px radius); not clickable = flat tag (8 px).

## 16. Safe areas
108 px margins at 1080 px width. 9:16 keeps top 250 px and bottom 340 px clear; 16:9 keeps bottom 140 px clear. Text at least 28 px at 1080 px width.

## 17. Motion
Calm and precise. Entrances expo.out / power4.out; text sharpens from blur in 0.16–0.3 s; thin lines draw in 0.6 s. Only what changes moves. No bounce or elastic.

## 18. Image treatment
Natural, slightly cool color, soft contrast. No heavy filters, HDR or neon.

## 19. Photography
The real team, never actors or stock. Soft daylight or a warm lamp in a dark room.

## 20. Illustration
None. Graphics are format cards, thin lines and the ring, the only 3D object.

## 21. Sound
The founder's own voice; understated electronic music under it; captions always; every ad works muted.

## 22. Product identity / protected details
Name, wordmark, upright ring, the offer wording "20% off your first month". Real screens and real work only.

## 23. Brand voice
A senior creative director talking to a peer: plain, calm, confident.

## 24. Tone
Client's goals first; people first, engine second. A light touch of wit at most.

## 25. Copy rules
Core words: concept, versions, engine, experts. Buttons start with a verb ("Start a test month"). Avoid: AI-powered, generate, unlimited, magic, revolutionary, exclamation marks.

## 26. Prohibitions
No altered logo; no tilted, spun or recolored ring; no stock or AI-looking images; no invented numbers or testimonials; no client names or logos; no shouty retail styling.

## 27. Accessibility
Body text contrast ≥ 4.5 : 1 (navy on white 14.7 : 1). Orange on white 3.3 : 1: large text only. Captions on every spoken video.

## 28. Localization
US English source. Inter Tight covers Latin, Cyrillic, Greek and Vietnamese; *proposed:* Noto Sans for other scripts. Leave 30% extra room for German and French.
