## 1 · The campaign

**Your website or app store page**
https://concepts.toggle.studio

**What are we advertising?**
Toggle Concepts is a creative department by subscription, from Toggle, the motion design studio.
- Senior creatives own each ad concept: the idea, the branding and the art direction.
- Our engine builds every version of it in formats, sizes, languages, hooks and calls to action.
- Experts check every file before delivery.
- Clients pay per concept; every version is included. Plans: Starter (2 concepts a month), Growth (4), Scale (6).

**The one benefit, in a sentence**
Test as many ads as you need without losing your look or quality.

**What should the ad achieve?**
Sign-ups: the viewer starts a test month by booking the first call.

**Who is it for?**
- **Main:** marketing, performance and brand leads at growing digital brands that run paid ads every month (apps, e-commerce, fintech, gaming). They need many fresh ad versions to keep tests running and struggle to keep the look consistent.
- **Second:** creative and performance agencies that need extra production capacity, white-label, inside their own pipeline.

**Where will it run?**
United Kingdom, European Union (English-language campaigns), United States.

**Offer**
20% off your first month

**Calls to action**
1. Start a test month (main)
2. Get 20% off your first month
3. See how it works

### More detail

**Why should people believe it?**
15+ years of motion and art direction at Toggle. First files within 5 days of the first call.

**What makes it different?**
People own the idea and the art direction, our engine builds every version, and experts check every file. Freelancers give you craft without volume; AI tools give you volume without craft. We give both.

**What gets in their way today?**
They need dozens of new ads every month to keep tests running. Freelancers and AI tools break the brand, and the in-house team cannot keep up.
